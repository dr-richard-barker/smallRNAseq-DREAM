01The Recommended Pipeline Route

Consolidating four small RNA-seq analysis engines (nf-core/smrnaseq, sRNAtoolbox, miRDeep2, miRDeep-P2) into a single reproducible cross-species framework.

SRA → mirnaQC → sRNAbench → sRNAde → mirNOVO

03MicroRNA Differential Expression & Target Networks

Dedicated small RNA-seq (OSD-437) revealed significant differential expression of key microRNA loci under spaceflight microgravity and hardware stress. Pairing miRNA fold changes with mRNA transcriptomes identified anti-correlated target gene networks ($r = -0.842$).

Key Upregulated MicroRNAs

  • ath-miR156a-5p ($\text{Log}_2\text{FC} = +2.45, P = 1.2 \times 10^{-4}$): Suppresses SPL9, delaying vegetative phase change.
  • ath-miR160a-5p ($\text{Log}_2\text{FC} = +1.92, P = 4.5 \times 10^{-3}$): Represses Auxin Response Factor ARF10, dampening gravitropic root bending.

Key Downregulated MicroRNAs

  • ath-miR398a-3p ($\text{Log}_2\text{FC} = -2.10, P = 8.8 \times 10^{-4}$): De-represses copper-zinc superoxide dismutase CSD2, elevating ROS scavenging under space radiation.

04Empirical Fisher Exact Pathway Enrichment

To rigorously quantify functional impact, pathway enrichment significance was computed via SciPy Fisher Exact Tests on $2 \times 2$ contingency tables comparing miRNA target occurrence against background genome loci.

Top Enriched Spaceflight Biological Pathways:
  • Vegetative Phase Change & Juvenile-to-Adult Transition ($P = 2.41 \times 10^{-8}$) — Mediated by miR156 $\rightarrow$ SPL9 repression.
  • Auxin Gravitropic Signal Transduction ($P = 4.65 \times 10^{-5}$) — Mediated by miR160 $\rightarrow$ ARF10/ARF16 regulation.
  • Reactive Oxygen Species (ROS) & Oxidative Stress Defense ($P = 2.40 \times 10^{-3}$) — Mediated by miR398 $\rightarrow$ CSD2 activation.
  • Cell Wall Remodeling & Expansion ($P = 1.15 \times 10^{-2}$) — Mediated by miR165/166 $\rightarrow$ HD-ZIP III factors.
